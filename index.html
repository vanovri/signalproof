Know what this test cannot tell you.

This prototype checks signal quality using declared rails, capture timestamps, and a Hann-windowed FFT. Thresholds are heuristics. A flag does not prove a hardware fault. No flags do not validate a sensor. Use only low-voltage bench recordings. This tool does not diagnose medical conditions or control hardware.

50 Hz analysis needs regular timestamps, at least one second, at least 120 Hz sample rate, and a retained FFT block with bin spacing at most 1 Hz. The FFT uses the first power-of-two block, so later changes may be missed. Synthetic examples demonstrate software behavior. Real hardware performance remains unvalidated.
